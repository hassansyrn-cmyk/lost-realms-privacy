Privacy Policy

Lost Realms: Open World (Android) · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Short version: Lost Realms: Open World is a single-player game that works offline. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. We run no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Your progress stays on your device.

1. Who we are

The game is made by an independent developer (“we”). You can contact us at hassan.syrn@gmail.com.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Game saves: your progress (hero name, currencies, quests, unlocked areas) is saved in the game's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.
  • Settings: your language, graphics and control preferences are saved locally.
  • Performance report (optional): the game can write a technical report of frame rate on your request. It leaves your phone only if you press Share and choose where to send it.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the game.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, crash-reporting services or advertising SDKs, and we do not use the Android Advertising ID.
  • We do not read your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.

4. Purchases

The game may offer optional in-app purchases (for example gems or a starter pack). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was completed, and keeps a purchase identifier on your device so that the same purchase is not granted twice. Google handles that information under Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The game has no chat, no user-generated content sharing and no social features. Because it does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

6. Deleting your data

All game data is in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes it. There is no copy on any server of ours.

7. Changes

If a future version adds online features, analytics or advertising, we will update this policy and the Google Play “Data safety” section before that version is released.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: hassan.syrn@gmail.com
